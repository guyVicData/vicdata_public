One layer ladder, three themes

✓Open panel = a sunk well, the chart’s own plate. Shut panel = a raised bar with a border, a shadow, a solid chevron and its lead figure in the accent: it reads as a button.✓Dark and light use the same ladder: page → column → well (open) / raised bar (shut). Each step is now clearly visible; today the shut bar is 6% from its column.✓Mid-tone: the page is Mint laid at 80% over near-black (#2ca165), Apple’s blend. The big title can be white; column headings and everything small is charcoal or sits on a white plate.✓Inside every plate the chart is unchanged: same keys, focus line, dashed average. Only the peer-line ambers deepen on white, as in light mode.✓In mid-tone the accent bar and column icon turn white/charcoal, because the page itself is now the accent. The focus line inside the plate stays green.
